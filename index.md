# Dream Valley — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 28, 2026*

Dream Valley ("the game") is made by Thach Nguyen ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and
why. Contact: **gamevui.thestone@gmail.com**

## What the game itself stores

- **Your progress** (coins, items, quests, outfits, achievements, settings) is saved **only on your device**. We do
  not run servers, and we do not receive or see your progress.
- The game has **no accounts** and asks for no name, email, contacts, photos or files.
- Uninstalling the game deletes this saved progress.

## Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. **An ad plays only when you choose to watch one** to earn coins. These
ads are provided by **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource Mobile Ltd., part of Unity Technologies). To show and measure ads
and prevent fraud, LevelPlay may collect:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers;
- **approximate location** (derived from your IP address);
- **interactions with ads** (for example, whether an ad was shown or clicked);
- **diagnostic information** (for example, crashes and performance).

This information is encrypted in transit and may be shared with advertising partners for the purposes above. You
can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or
Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). For more, see Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

On iPhone, the game first asks Apple's permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the advertising
ID is not shared and ads are not personalised; you can still watch ads for coins. You can change this any time in
Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## In-app purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

Coin packs are bought through **Google Play** or Apple's **App Store**. Payment details are handled entirely by Google
or Apple; we never see your card or payment information. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ·
Apple's: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices and requests

- To stop ad data collection, don't watch ads; you can also opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
- To ask about, or request deletion of, data collected by the advertising partner, contact us at the email above
  and we will pass the request to Unity, or contact Unity directly via its privacy policy.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
